CardSelector

Which card should I use here?

I carry several cards. Each has a changing list of bonus offers at different shops. I lose track of which card to use where. I search for a shop in CardSelector. It combines each card’s standing rate with any current offer there and picks my best card for that purchase. I can see which offers need activation, plus any minimum spend, caps and exclusions. If I only know the category, I use quick picks to find the best standing rate. I check it on my phone just before I pay.

Interactive demo

This demo uses invented cards and offers. It connects to nothing and never updates. None of the sample offers are current. My private app uses only my own cards. Click or tap inside the screen to take over.

What it does

An offer is only part of the answer. The card’s usual rate counts too. CardSelector puts the two together and keeps a dollar reward beside the minimum spend it takes to earn it.
